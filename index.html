Before you apply

The questions everyone asks

I travel and eat out constantly. Is this going to work?

It's the reason the program exists. You get a restaurant playbook covering steakhouse, sushi, airport and the place with nothing on the menu, plus a travel structure with hotel and low-equipment training. Work dinners are part of the plan, not a break from it.

How does online coaching actually work?

Everything runs through your phone. Training and video demos live in the app, your nutrition plan arrives as a document, check-ins are a short weekly form, and messaging is direct to me. No gym visits, no fixed session times, no time zone problem.

How much time do I need in the gym?

Three to four sessions a week, 40–60 minutes. Every session also has a compressed version for days that collapse. Consistency at 40 minutes outperforms perfection at ninety.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on the length of the engagement and is covered on the call. Coaching is a considered purchase, and the application exists so neither of us spends time on a bad fit.

What happens after I apply?

You'll answer a short set of questions about your goals, schedule and history. If it looks like a fit, you'll get a link to book a call with me directly. If it isn't, I'll tell you that and point you somewhere better.

Your coach

The full story

Seven years ago, I started posting my fitness journey every single day.

At the time, I had no idea where it would lead. I was bouncing between jobs, trying different businesses, and searching for something I could truly build my life around. The one thing I always came back to was fitness.

But my relationship with fitness wasn't always healthy. For years, I trained like a bodybuilder and built my entire life around the gym. No balance. No real structure. Eating out made me feel guilty. Missing a workout felt like failure. I could be in great shape physically while mentally feeling completely lost.

There were periods where I was depressed, uncertain about my future, and putting everything I had into becoming better physically because it was one of the few things I felt like I could control.

Eventually, I realized I didn't want fitness to be my entire life. I wanted fitness to make my life better.

That's when I discovered hybrid training and everything started to change. I learned how to lift, run, eat, travel, go out, build a business, and still make progress — without the constant guilt of feeling like I was doing something wrong.

And after seven years of documenting my own journey, that became the foundation of Hybrid Life Coaching. You shouldn't have to choose between being in incredible shape and actually enjoying your life. The goal is to build both.

Myles Harris · Founder, Hybrid Life Coaching

Phase 01 · Weeks 1–2

Audit

Before anything changes, we find out what's actually happening. Most people aren't failing their plan — they're on the wrong one for their real energy expenditure.

  • Full TDEE and intake audit against your actual week
  • Baseline photos, measurements, lifts, sleep and stress
  • Your calendar mapped — travel, dinners, busy seasons

Phase 02 · Weeks 3–8

Build the system

The structure goes in. This phase is about making the plan survive a bad week, not about maximum aggression.

  • Custom nutrition document and training block built around your schedule
  • Restaurant, travel and weekend playbooks written before you need them
  • Weekly check-ins start; we adjust off data, not feel

Phase 03 · Weeks 9–16

Push

Now we go after the number you've been stuck at. Calories cycle with your week, training intensity climbs, and the plateau breaks.

  • Carb cycling and day-type structure matched to training load
  • Progressive overload with a short version of every session
  • Mindset work on the decisions that used to derail you

Phase 04 · Week 17 onward

Make it permanent

The phase most programs skip, and the reason most results don't last. You leave able to run this yourself.

  • Calories brought back up deliberately instead of by accident
  • Move from weighing food to portions you can eyeball anywhere
  • You learn the why behind every adjustment, so you can make them alone
